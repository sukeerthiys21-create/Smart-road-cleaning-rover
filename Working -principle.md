# Working Principle

The Smart Road Cleaning Rover is a compact, human-controlled cleaning system designed to collect roadside waste without disturbing traffic or pedestrians.

## 1. Start
The operator switches ON the rover and checks the battery, motors, sensors, and waste container.

## 2. Movement
The rover moves using DC geared motors. It can move forward, backward, left, and right according to the operator's control.

## 3. Waste Detection
Sensors help the operator identify obstacles and maintain a safe distance from people, vehicles, and roadside objects.

## 4. Waste Collection
A rotating brush or collection mechanism gathers small roadside waste and directs it into the attached waste container.

## 5. Obstacle Safety
When an obstacle is detected, the rover can stop or the operator can change its direction to avoid collision.

## 6. Waste Container
Collected waste is stored in a removable container. The container can be emptied easily when it becomes full.

## 7. Human Control
The rover remains under human supervision, allowing the operator to control its movement and cleaning path.

## 8. Main Objective
The system aims to provide a compact, affordable, durable, easy-to-maintain, and practical method for cleaning roads and public areas without blocking traffic.
