# Smart Road Cleaning Rover

## Project Overview

A compact, affordable and durable smart rover designed to collect and remove waste from roads and public areas.

The rover is designed to make cleaning easier while minimizing disturbance to pedestrians, vehicles and surrounding property.

## Main Goal

To develop a practical cleaning system that can be controlled by a human operator and adapted for use in different public areas.

## Key Objectives

- Collect common roadside waste efficiently.
- Operate without blocking or disturbing traffic.
- Be compact and easy to maintain.
- Keep the design affordable and durable.
- Make the system adaptable to different environments.
