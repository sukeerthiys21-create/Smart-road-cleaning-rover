# Safety Considerations – Smart Road Cleaning Rover

## 1. Electrical Safety

- Use components with compatible voltage and current ratings.
- Check all wiring before powering on the rover.
- Avoid short circuits and loose connections.
- Disconnect the battery before changing circuit connections.

## 2. Battery Safety

- Use a suitable battery and charger.
- Do not use damaged, swollen, or leaking batteries.
- Keep batteries away from water and excessive heat.
- Follow the battery manufacturer's instructions.

## 3. Mechanical Safety

- Keep fingers, hair, and loose clothing away from moving wheels and brushes.
- Secure all motors and components firmly.
- Switch off the rover before maintenance.

## 4. Operating Safety

- Test the rover in a clear, controlled area.
- Keep it away from traffic, stairs, and crowded areas.
- Supervise testing and stop the rover if it behaves unexpectedly.

## 5. Environmental Considerations

- Avoid collecting hazardous, sharp, or unknown waste.
- Dispose of collected waste responsibly.
- Prevent battery or electronic waste from entering the environment.
